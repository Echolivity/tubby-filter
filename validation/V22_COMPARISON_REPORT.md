## 3. V2.1 regression (V2.2 with Motion = 0)
Each comparison uses the same input, parameters and sample rate on both versions, compared with `numpy.array_equal` on the float64 outputs, which is a genuine bit-level comparison.

**Report data:** 7 signal types × 5 rates = 35 cases, **35 / 35 bit-identical**. In every case the RMS error is 0, the peak error is 0, the residual is −∞ dBFS (shown as −300) and the maximum spectral difference is 0.

| Signal type | 44.1 | 48 | 88.2 | 96 | 192 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Static cutoff (1 kHz, Q 1.4, pink) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Cutoff sweeps (random steps, Glide 300) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Resonance sweep 0.5 → 8 | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Impulse (500 Hz, Q 4) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Step / DC response (100 Hz, Q 8) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Transients (dub loop, hard steps, Click 80) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |
| Gain (Drive 90 Peak, Out −7, Mix 63) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |

**Additional regression tests** (`tests/test_v22.py`, all PASS [sim]):
- R01–R08: 8 scenarios × 5 rates, each run with Motion omitted *and* with Motion explicitly 0. R08 automates all 12 V2.1 controls, including device on/off.
- R09: identical across sample-rate changes.
- R10: after Motion has been used and returned to 0, the output matches V2.1 within 1e-9 two seconds later.
- R11: reference model ↔ source bit-exact with everything automated.

**Phase, gain and impulse** are covered by bit-identity: identical sample sequences have identical phase, gain and frequency response.

**Audio null:** `reports/v22/audio/null_v22_motion0_minus_v21_48k.wav` (V2.2 at Motion 0 minus V2.1 on the dub loop with performed automation). Its peak is **0.0 exactly**.

**V2.1 behaviour suite on V2.2:** the full V2.1 regression suite (`tests/test_regression.py`) runs unchanged against V2.2. Results are in `V22_TEST_RESULTS.md`.

## 4. Frequency response
- **Static response is unaffected by Motion.** At Motion **100** with a static cutoff, every one of 11 steps × 2 slopes × 3 Q values gives an impulse response bit-identical to V2.1, at all 5 rates.
- **Worst deviation from the analog prototype** (unchanged from V2.1): 1.010 dB at 44.1/88.2 kHz, 0.848 dB at 48/96 kHz and 0.208 dB at 192 kHz. In each case it's the 10 kHz step.
- **Plot:** `reports/v22/img/frequency_response.png` (magnitude and unwrapped phase, all steps, 18 dB, Q 1.4, 48 kHz).

## 5. Stability (rc1)

| Test | 44.1 | 48 | 96 | 192 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every control randomised every sample, including Motion, ±4 noise | finite, peak +6.6 dBFS | finite, +5.6 | finite, +6.7 | finite, +7.5 |
| NaN, ±Inf and 1e30 input, Q 8, Motion 100, Drive 100 | finite, +8.0 (safety ceiling) | finite, +8.0 | finite, +8.0 | finite, +8.0 |

These tests are also in the suite (S01–S09):
- maximum resonance ringing down at every step;
- silence followed by a sudden transient;
- input at 0, +12 and +24 dB;
- 10 s of sustained 30 Hz at 0.9 FS with no level growth (±0.1 dB) and no DC;
- DC input;
- every control flipped at the same instant;
- NaN and Inf;
- denormals (no subnormal output);
- **60 s of a repeating 2 s performance pattern: no drift (period levels within ±0.05 dB) and no DC.**
